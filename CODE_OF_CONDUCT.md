# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The Nexment Project exists to build, learn, share, and collaborate.

We want every person interacting with Nexment to feel safe, respected, and welcome — regardless of their background, experience, identity, or perspective.

Participation in Nexment comes with a responsibility to treat others with dignity.

Nobody should be made to feel unsafe, threatened, harassed, or deliberately humiliated for participating in Nexment.

## Expected Behavior

Everyone participating in Nexment is expected to:

- Treat others with respect and courtesy.
- Welcome people with different backgrounds, experiences, and perspectives.
- Assume good faith when discussing ideas and contributions.
- Give constructive feedback without attacking the person behind the work.
- Respect different opinions and approaches.
- Keep discussions productive and relevant.
- Accept mistakes as part of learning and development.
- Give appropriate credit to the work of others.
- Respect people's privacy and personal boundaries.

Technical disagreement is welcome.

Personal attacks are not.

Strong opinions are welcome.

Harassment is not.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not tolerated anywhere within Nexment:

- Harassment, bullying, or intimidation.
- Discrimination or hateful conduct.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberate humiliation.
- Threats of violence or harm.
- Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing or distributing someone's private information without permission.
- Trolling or deliberately disrupting discussions or projects.
- Repeated unwanted contact or targeting of individuals.
- Plagiarism, impersonation, or deliberate misrepresentation of another person's work.
- Retaliation against someone who reports misconduct.
- Any other behavior that reasonably makes another person feel unsafe, threatened, or unwelcome.

## Disagreements

Disagreement is normal.

Challenge ideas, code, designs, decisions, and technical approaches — not the person behind them.

Keep discussions focused on the subject rather than turning disagreements into personal conflicts.

Being technically correct does not justify being disrespectful.

## Reporting a Complaint

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to:

**contact@nexment.in**

For sensitive matters, you may report privately rather than creating a public issue or discussion.

Please provide as much relevant information as you safely can, including what happened, where it happened, and any relevant evidence.

Reports will be taken seriously and handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported according to the security reporting process provided by Nexment.

## Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in immediate action by The Nexment Project.

Depending on the circumstances and severity of the violation, enforcement may include removal of the offending content, restriction of access, or complete removal of the individual from Nexment.

A person removed from Nexment may be:

- Removed from Nexment projects and repositories.
- Removed from Nexment websites and platforms.
- Removed from Nexment-created programs, courses, and other creations.
- Removed from relevant Nexment databases and services, where appropriate.
- Blocked from participating in Nexment communities.
- Prevented from contributing to or representing Nexment.

For serious violations, threats, illegal activity, or matters involving potential criminal conduct, Nexment may report the matter to the appropriate legal authorities.

Retaliation against anyone who makes a good-faith complaint is strictly prohibited.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to **every part of The Nexment Project**.

This includes, but is not limited to:

- Nexment websites and services.
- GitHub repositories and organizations.
- Nexment Dev Community.
- Nexment Academy.
- Courses and educational content.
- Documentation and blogs.
- Social media accounts and official channels.
- YouTube and other media.
- Projects, software, and open-source contributions.
- Events and community activities.
- Any other platform, service, creation, or space officially operated or represented by Nexment.

It also applies when someone is representing Nexment in public or community spaces.

## Final Note

Nexment is built by people.

Be respectful.  
Be curious.  
Be honest.  
Build things.  
Help others build them too.

**There is always a person on the other side of the screen.**
